How it works

What it does

Forever Sim plays your character through the same fights thousands of times. Every swing, spell, critical hit, dodge and proc is a dice roll, just as in the game, so no two fights go quite the same way. Averaging them shows what a build really does.

Set up one to three builds, press Simulate, and each build plays through every test: leveling with one, two or three mobs at a time, a dungeon boss, two world PvP ambushes, and duels between your builds. Everything runs on your own computer; nothing is uploaded.

Two more tabs run bigger jobs. Class rankings puts every build on the site through three tests and ranks them, and Duels pits one build against every other, both ways round.

Reading the results

  • The scorecard has one row per test and one column per build. Open a row to see that test in detail.
  • The baseline is the build the others are measured against. Any build can be the baseline; use its card’s menu.
  • “About the same” means the gap is smaller than the simulation’s margin of error. Every build faces exactly the same dice rolls, so a clear gap comes from the builds, not luck.
  • Ranges are 95% confidence intervals: if you ran the test again, the average would almost always land inside them.

Trying a change

Press “Add a build to compare” to copy a build, change something in the copy, and simulate. After you change a build and simulate again, the results say what your change did, measured on the same fights as before.

What it cannot tell you

Talents marked with a grey corner are not simulated yet and change nothing. A few numbers the game does not publish are guesses; you can change them under Assumptions. The simulated player follows its play style perfectly, so real results are usually a little lower.
