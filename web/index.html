How it works

What it does

Forever Sim plays your character through the same fight thousands of times. Every swing, spell, critical hit, dodge and proc is a dice roll, just as in the game, so no two fights go quite the same way. Averaging them shows what the build really does.

Pick a class, race and level, start from a ready-made build or spend your own talent points, then choose a test and press Simulate. Everything runs on your own computer; nothing is uploaded.

Reading the results

  • The headline number is the average over every simulated fight.
  • “Likely between” is a 95% confidence interval: if you ran the test again, the average would almost always land inside it.
  • Comparisons are fair. Every build faces exactly the same dice rolls, so a gap between builds comes from the builds, not luck. When a gap is smaller than the margin of error, the result says the builds are too close to call.

What it cannot tell you

Talents marked with a grey corner are not simulated yet and change nothing. A few numbers the game does not publish are guesses; you can change them under Assumptions. The simulated player follows its play style perfectly, so real results are usually a little lower.

Comparing builds

Press “Compare with another build” to copy the current build, change something in the copy, and simulate. You can compare up to three builds. “Share these builds” copies a link with all of them.
